# Portfolio design direction

Concept: an editorial field notebook meets a film contact sheet.
Audience: journalism and creative-media editors, graduate admissions reviewers.
Primary job: identify the work quickly, then open a readable project detail.

## Commitments

- Focal point: the actual work covers, never decorative stock imagery.
- Tension move: a loose cover wall on the opening spread, measured grids thereafter.
- Signature asset: the original project cover appears in the opening wall, work index, and detail.
- Uncommon decision: the orange journalism chapter encloses a calm paper work surface.
- Deliberate exception: film posters retain their native vertical aspect within the shared grid.

## Tokens

- Paper `#F4F0E8`; ink `#1C1B18`; stone `#EAE4D8`.
- Vermilion `#DF552F` is the chapter field; deeper `#BD4128` carries small accent text and controls. No gradients.
- Display: Iowan Old Style, Georgia fallback; body: Avenir Next, Helvetica Neue fallback; labels: Menlo, monospace fallback.
- Display 88–132px desktop / 54–72px mobile, compact leading and negative tracking.
- Body 16–18px with 1.45–1.6 line height; metadata 11–12px.
- Grid: 12 columns within a maximum 1344px measure; 24px mobile gutter.
- Rules: 1px ink at reduced opacity; square corners; no decorative cards.
- Motion: 240ms cover lift and arrow move; smooth anchor scroll. Disable under reduced motion.

## Page rhythm

- Cover fills one viewport below the header: centered orange title at left, enlarged real-cover collage at right. Images use intrinsic height at every breakpoint; the collage is above its background and may extend beyond its frame.
- Journalism: orange chapter header, paper work sheet, one lead plus two balanced pairs.
- Creative media: pale chapter, the same paper work sheet and lead-plus-pairs structure. The final Shanghai poster is centered rather than edge-aligned.
- Visual archive: pale ground and matching paper sheet, horizontal internship rail, denser photography grid.
- Section rails, archive explanatory captions, the hero period, and footer copyright are removed as requested.
- Detail: full-screen editorial reading view; original media immediately below description.

Preserve every project title, role, award, image, video, link, and existing detail interaction. No synthetic imagery or invented copy.
